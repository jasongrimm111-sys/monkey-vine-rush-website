Privacy policy

Monkey Vine Rush

Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version

  • No tracking, no chat, no adverts during play.
  • The game doesn't collect names, email addresses, locations or any other personal information.
  • Coin purchases are optional, go through Apple or Google, and need a grown-up to answer a question first.
  • If you email us, we only use your message to help you. Questions? Email support@monkeyvinerush.com.

Who we are

Monkey Vine Rush is a game made by Jason Grimmitt, trading as Grimmitt Games, in the United Kingdom. Jason is responsible for any personal information described in this policy (the "data controller"). You can reach him at support@monkeyvinerush.com. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores

  • Your progress: coins, stars, levels, items, missions, badges and settings. This is saved on your device so you can carry on where you left off.
  • An online copy of your progress, if you play while signed in to the service that hosts the game, so your progress can be restored on another device. It is linked to your account there and holds only game progress. In the app store versions, this copy is kept in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account instead.
  • Ghost replays of your best runs. These stay on your device only.

What the game does not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not use advertising IDs. There are no third-party analytics or tracking, no chat and no social features, and the only adverts are the optional videos described below. We do not sell or share your information.

When you email us

If you email us, for example to ask for help or to send a problem report from the game, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write or attach. A problem report lists your device type, the game's version and settings, where you were in the game and any errors it noticed. It contains nothing else about you.

We use your email only to reply to you and to fix problems in the game. Emails reach us through an email forwarding service (ImprovMX) and are kept in our email account (Gmail, run by Google). We keep support emails for up to two years and then delete them, and we'll delete them sooner if you ask.

Purchases

Buying coins is never required. Everything in the game can be unlocked with coins earned by playing. Buying coins is optional and simply gives you a faster way to unlock worlds, monkeys and items.

Coin packs are in-app purchases made through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase went through, never your card or payment details.

Coins can only be used in the game. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their refund policies.

You can turn off or limit in-app purchases in your device's settings, for example with a password or parental controls.

Optional videos

In the app, you can choose to watch a short video advert to get free coins or a free power-up. A video only plays when you tap to watch one, and there are never adverts during play.

The videos come from Google AdMob. We ask Google to treat every request as coming from a child: the adverts are family-friendly, never personalised, and do not use your advertising ID or track you across apps. To show a video and count that it was watched, Google receives technical information such as your device type, the app's version and your IP address (used for your approximate country), under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The web version has no adverts.

Leaderboards and sharing

In the app store versions, if you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, your best Rush and Boss Rush distances and your star total can be posted to their leaderboards. Those services show your player name there under their own privacy policies.

The Share button makes a picture of your result and opens your device's share options. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Before coins can be bought, a grown-up has to answer a quick question.

Why we can use it

Under UK and EU data protection law, we save your game progress and answer your emails because we have a legitimate interest in running the game and helping players, and we handle coin purchases to carry out our agreement with you (Apple or Google process the payment). We never use your information for profiling, adverts are never personalised, and we make no automated decisions about you.

Fonts

The game's fonts are built into the game, so it does not contact any font service or other third party when it loads.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is designed to protect children's privacy by default: there are no accounts, chat or tracking, it collects no personal information, and the only adverts are optional, family-friendly videos that are never personalised. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age set by the law where they live. If a child emails us, we use the message only to reply, and we encourage children to ask a parent or guardian before contacting us. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. Email us and we'll reply within one month. A parent or guardian can make these requests for their child. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your device. If you use iCloud, your progress is also saved in your own iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices, and it comes back if you reinstall the app. You can turn this off for Monkey Vine Rush in your device's iCloud settings. We don't keep a copy of your progress on our own servers. The online copies are kept by Apple in your iCloud account and Game Center, and Apple manages them. You can see or delete your Apple data at privacy.apple.com.

Keeping it safe and where it's stored

Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account, protected by Apple's or Google's security. Our email account is protected with two-step verification. The services we use, such as Google and ImprovMX, may store emails outside the UK, including in the United States, with the safeguards the law requires. We keep as little information as possible, for as short a time as possible.

Our website

If you accept analytics cookies on monkeyvinerush.com, we use Google Analytics to see how the site is used, such as which pages are viewed, the type of device and browser, and the approximate country or city. Google processes this for us under its own privacy policy and keeps it for no more than 14 months. We only do this with your consent, which you can change at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of any page. The site shows no adverts. See our cookie policy.

Translations

The game's text in languages other than English was translated with the help of AI. Translations may contain mistakes or read differently from the English, and if there is any difference, the English version applies. We are not responsible for errors in translated text.

Names and trademarks

All names, characters, monkeys, outfits, hats, items, worlds and other content in the game, including everything in the shop, are original creations made for Monkey Vine Rush. Any resemblance to real people, brands, products, trademarks or copyrighted characters is coincidental and not intended. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any trademark or copyright owner. Apple and Google are named only to explain how app store payments work, and their names belong to their owners.
