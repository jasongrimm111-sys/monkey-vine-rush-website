Cookie policy

Monkey Vine Rush

Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version

  • Analytics cookies are only used if you accept them.
  • No advertising cookies, and no tracking across other sites.
  • If you pick a language, your browser remembers it on your device.

Cookies

If you accept, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It sets these cookies:

  • _ga: tells one visitor from another (kept for 2 years)
  • _ga_2M1YFDZQH2: keeps track of your current visit (kept for 2 years)

Google Analytics records things like the pages you view, your type of device and browser, and roughly where you are (worked out from your IP address, which isn’t stored). We don’t use it to identify you, for advertising, or to follow you on other sites. If you reject, or don’t choose, these cookies are never set and Google Analytics doesn’t load at all.

Your choice

Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage (under the name mvr-consent). You can change it at any time here, or with Cookie settings at the bottom of any page.

Your language choice

When you choose a language, the site saves that choice in your browser's local storage (under the name mvr-lang) so it opens in your language next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. It's only saved because you asked for it, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Fonts and hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, when your browser loads the pages and fonts, these services receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type. They don't set cookies for this site. You can read how they handle data in the GitHub privacy statement and the Google privacy policy.

The game

The Monkey Vine Rush app doesn’t use cookies. See our privacy policy for what the game stores.

Changes and contact
