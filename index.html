Why you'll love it

Easy to learn.
Impossible to put down.

Every swing feels amazing, and there's always one more level, one more banana and one more world to unlock.

Hold & let go

The perfect swing, every time

Press anywhere to grab a vine, build up your swing, then let go at just the right moment to soar. Tap in mid-air for a Power Spin. Prefer buttons? Switch on the Swing and Spin buttons and put them wherever suits your thumbs.

  • Build momentum and fly sky high
  • Swoop low for hidden bananas
  • Earn three stars on every level
24 worlds

From jungles to outer space

Frosty peaks, bubbling volcanoes, candy kingdoms, coral reefs, haunted hollows, neon cities and more, each with its own weather, hazards and a boss to beat.

  • 50 hand-built levels in every world
  • Blizzards, sandstorms, fog and thunder
  • A brand-new challenge every day
Rush mode

Run! The chaser is right behind you

In endless Rush, a jaguar, a yeti, a lava wave and more are hot on your tail. Swing as far as you can, grab power-ups on the way and beat your best.

  • Rocket Boost and Chaser Freeze
  • Weather that changes as you go
  • Race your own best run
Boss battles

Face down epic bosses

Every 10th level ends in a showdown. Swing straight into the boss to land a hit, Power Spin for double damage, and dodge its fireballs, icicles and lasers. Every world has its own boss, from Big Beak the toucan to the Crystal Dragon.

  • A boss battle every 10 levels
  • Dodge fireballs, icicles and lasers
  • 24 bosses, each with its own attacks
Boss Rush

Every boss, one after another

Unlock all 24 worlds to open Boss Rush: one endless run through every world, with a boss waiting in each. Beat it to swing on to the next. How many can you take down?

  • All 24 bosses in a single run
  • Bigger coin rewards for every boss
  • Its own Game Center leaderboard
Make it yours

Dress up your monkey

Unlock new monkeys, each with their own perks, then mix and match outfits, hats, swings and sparkly trails. Super Cape, Royal Crown, Rainbow Grapple… you choose.

  • Monkeys with special abilities
  • Dozens of outfits and hats
  • Earn coins just by playing
Every day

Something new every day

Open a daily gift, take on the daily challenge, complete missions and collect badges. There's always a reason to come back for one more swing.

  • Daily gifts and a 7-day streak
  • Missions and dozens of badges
  • Game Center leaderboards

Made for families

Fun you can feel good about

No intrusive ads

No pop-ups or adverts during play. Watch a short video only if you want free coins or a power-up.

Plays offline

Perfect for car rides, flights and anywhere without Wi-Fi.

No accounts

No sign-ups, and no personal data collected.

Saves with iCloud

Pick up where you left off on your iPhone or iPad.
